What it is today, plainly

We would rather you judge it on a real pilot than on a sales page.

  • Grounded on your own fact sheet. Suggestions are built from the material you give us, so the agent is not handed invented prices or dates. If something is not in the sheet, it stays out of the reply.
  • It is a pilot. DealWhisper is early. We set it up by hand for each team and improve it with your feedback during the trial.
  • The agent stays in charge. It suggests, it never speaks to the buyer. Your agent decides what to say.
  • Speed depends on where it runs. The demo ran on an ordinary laptop processor, so suggestions took 7 to 17 seconds and the video shows that wait at 2x. Pilots run on faster hardware.
